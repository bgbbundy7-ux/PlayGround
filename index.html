REACH PROTOCOL

Orbital Defense — a Halo: Reach–inspired 3D FPS rebuilt for iPhone & iPad. Hold the ridge against waves of Krell invaders. Shields regen. Ammo is scarce. Aim for the head.

OPERATIONS · 10 MISSIONS + ENDLESS
iPhone / iPad: left stick = move (full tilt = sprint) · drag right side = look · FIRE = shoot — hold FIRE and drag to aim + shoot one-thumb · AIM = zoom · JUMP / CRCH / RLD / SWAP / NADE buttons on the right (CRCH while sprinting = slide). Rearrange everything in 🎮 TOUCH LAYOUT.
🎮 Controller (Xbox / PlayStation / Switch Pro / MFi — pair via Bluetooth, then press any button): left stick = move (full tilt = sprint) · right stick = look · RT = fire · LT = aim · A = jump · X = reload · Y = swap weapon · B / RB = grenade · LB = swap · R3 = crouch/slide · Start = pause.
Mac / PC: WASD + mouse, click to lock, LMB shoot, RMB aim, R reload, Q swap, E/F grenade, Space jump, C crouch (sprint + C = slide), Shift sprint.
Shields recharge after 4s out of fire. Headshots ×2. Plasma lance eats shields. Green ammo crates drop between waves.
